# Velvet Luxury Digital Wedding Invitation (Continuous Vertical Scroll)
### தயாளன் & மர்யம் — திருமண வரவேற்பு அழைப்பிதழ்
**Inspired by [Wedsmint Velvet](https://wedsmint.com/velvet/)**

---

## 🌟 Overview

A luxury mobile-first digital wedding invitation replicated from the **Wedsmint Velvet** theme, tailored for the interfaith celebration of **தயாளன் (Thayalan)** & **மர்யம் (Maryam)**.

### ✨ What's New:
* **Continuous Top-to-Bottom Scroll:** The entire invitation now scrolls naturally and smoothly from top to bottom, exactly like the original Wedsmint Velvet site.
* **No Footer / Slider Bar:** Removed all bottom navigation bars and sliders for a clean, immersive full-screen experience.
* **Scroll-Reveal Animations:** Each section elegantly fades in as you scroll down.
* **Floral Dividers:** Ornate golden floral dividers separate each section.

---

## 📄 Continuous Sections (Top to Bottom)

1. **Opening Hero (Wax Seal Envelope):** Tap or scroll down on the royal seal `T & M - OPEN` to begin the journey with background shehnai music.
2. **Section 1 (அழைப்பிதழ் அட்டை - Invitation Card):** The attached wedding invitation card with gold shimmer sweep, 1-tap zoom lightbox, and calendar save.
3. **Section 2 (ஜோடி புகைப்படம் - Couple Portrait):** Arched portrait framed with hanging lanterns, bilingual romantic quotes, and wedding date ribbon.
4. **Section 3 (மண்டப வழித்தடம் - Venue & QR Code):** Live Google Maps QR code, address details (**காஜா மஹால், அபிஷேகப்பட்டி, திருநெல்வேலி**), 1-tap **Open in Google Maps**, **Copy Address**, and **Download QR**.
5. **Section 4 (நிகழ்வு நாள் & வாழ்த்து - Countdown & RSVP):** Live countdown timer to **Saturday, 28 November 2026**, family blessings verse, and 1-tap **WhatsApp RSVP / வாழ்த்து அனுப்புங்கள்**.
